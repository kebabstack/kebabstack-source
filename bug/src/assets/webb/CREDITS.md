# Webb photographs in Ship the Bug

These are genuine Webb image fields, used as scenery in a fictional game, not a
full-sky map. Distributed JPEGs retain the source bytes. Runtime display crops,
dims, blends and gently shifts them for the scene. No endorsement is implied.

- **carina.jpg — Cosmic Cliffs, Carina Nebula (NIRCam)**. Credit: NASA, ESA, CSA, and STScI.
  [Source](https://esawebb.org/images/weic2205a/) · [Official landscape JPEG](https://cdn.esawebb.org/archives/images/wallpaper4/weic2205a.jpg), 1920×1200.
- **phantom-galaxy.jpg — Phantom Galaxy M74 (Webb MIRI)**. Credit: ESA/Webb, NASA & CSA, J. Lee and the PHANGS-JWST Team. Acknowledgement: J. Schmidt.
  [Source](https://esawebb.org/images/potm2208a/) · [Official JPEG](https://cdn.esawebb.org/archives/images/large/potm2208a.jpg), 1977×1130.
- **deep-field.jpg — SMACS 0723, Webb First Deep Field (NIRCam)**. Credit: NASA, ESA, CSA, and STScI.
  [Source](https://esawebb.org/images/weic2209a/) · [Official landscape JPEG](https://cdn.esawebb.org/archives/images/wallpaper4/weic2209a.jpg), 1920×1200.
