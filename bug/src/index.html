 FLIGHT SCHOOL / ECOSYSTEM ATLAS

From Zürich to infinity.

  1. Choose your angle.A low launch travels fast. A high launch buys airtime.
  2. Hold. Release. Hope.Hold SPACE or the green button. Release near full for a perfect launch; the meter swings back if you hold too long.
  3. Steer through the good stuff.On a computer, steer with A / D or the left and right arrow keys. Mouse movement never steers the bug. On your phone, choose tilt or arrows before the first flight. Allow motion and hold comfortably to set neutral. The phone icon on the left edge switches tilt off or on. Pause and resume to reset your neutral grip. Rotating the screen pauses your flight and recalibrates. Arrows always work as a fallback; Boost and Fire stay on touch buttons.
  4. Five prompts. Use them wisely.Press SPACE or the green button mid-flight to boost. Every launch remixes the route. Gold rings are coins: every coin adds 50 points to your distance score. Coffee, compiler crystals and gates boost. Red firewalls cost serious momentum. Exploit mines guard the floor and some hover in the air. Their red rings mark danger: find a gap, fly over or clear one with a pulse. A mine hit costs 40% momentum; an Identity shield absorbs one hazard hit. Orange armor needs two pulses. Lift pickups get weaker as pressure rises; floor rescues are lucky finds. The glowing data stream carries you into cyberspace.
  5. Find your FLOW.Each coin adds 4 FLOW; every fifth coin in a series adds 8 more. Keep coins within 2.5 seconds of each other for the series. A genuinely close pass of a mine or firewall adds 20 FLOW after you clear it. At 100, a 3.2-second Overdrive starts automatically with extra thrust and a gold/mint trail. Hits break your FLOW; a shield protects it. A five-second recovery prevents endless Overdrive chains. Your five prompt boosts stay separate.
  6. Outsmart the Motoko ghost.The armoured Motoko ghost joins at 500 m, circles and follows into canister airspace. From 2,200 m it returns on later patrols, with quiet stretches between encounters. It dives in, charges a pink cannon for 0.8 seconds, fires two fast tracers at the locked lane, then climbs away for another pass. Dodge sideways or hit Motoko with two pulses. Only a projectile hit costs 33% momentum; touching its body does not. Debugging it buys eight seconds of peace and 75 K simulated cycles.
  7. Patch the firewall. With lasers.Hold F or J to shoot, or use the FIRE button on your phone. Nearby firewalls, mines and Motoko get a little aim assistance. Aim with your steering and fire in bursts: holding FIRE overheats the blaster. A target outline means lock-on. Each cleared firewall gives 25 K simulated cycles and a mine gives 15 K; these do not add leaderboard points. Releasing FIRE cools the weapon. Mission control appears briefly at a new epoch or when you need a last-chance boost. The bug stops its final crawl below 6 m/s; you have 1.6 seconds to use a remaining boost. Wind is announced before it arrives; tailwinds and updrafts can save a run.
