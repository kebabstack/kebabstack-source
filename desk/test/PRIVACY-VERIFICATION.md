## Deliberate limits

Deletion covers active Desk application records. It is not a secure-wipe or historical-backup guarantee. Old snapshots, earlier 0.11 delivered notification copies and exports require the documented operator process. The 365-day journal contains restricted pseudonymous references; digest retry markers also expire after 365 days. Journal export/replay is implemented and identity-checked, but this test run does not certify a production snapshot-restore isolation procedure. A normal Vault restore can restart a canister: operators must isolate access and reconcile a newer, separately retained journal before reopening, or expire old backups instead.

Deploy only through the authorized release-bundle/executor workflow. Verify actual hosted widget/private routes, privacy URL, origin rules and company CSP before real customer intake. No GDPR compliance certification is implied; the company chooses and justifies its policy.
