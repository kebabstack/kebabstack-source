- Standard: 1.2.0; module: Desk 0.27.0; reviewer: Codex, 2026-09-29.
- Status: verified for the local scope below. Not deployed.
- Primary users: Desk agents and admins. Employees and customer requests excluded.
- Rules: COMPONENTS-05/06/07, FORMS-02/06, DATA-02/03, STATES-02/03/04,
  NAVIGATION-06, ACCESSIBILITY-02/04, GOVERNANCE-07.

  After: select rows or all matching results, review one explicit action and count,
  then inspect the per-request result. No measured time or savings claim.
