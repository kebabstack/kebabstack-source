## Limits and next work

This is not a WCAG conformance certification or a VoiceOver audit. The full
screen-reader, zoom, text-spacing and app-role acceptance matrix remains part of
per-surface review. Token-pair checks do not certify arbitrary rendered colour
combinations. No phone delivery, external integration or live SSO was exercised
by the static documentation examples.

The new palette is a documented target, not a replacement already applied to
runtime apps. Follow `ADOPTION.md` for the shared-token/sign-in review and the
subsequent app waves. No app is marked conformant merely because these guidelines
exist. No live roles, directory/Lunch data, settings or business records changed.
