# Falcon in internal support

Standard 1.2.0. Hub 0.46.0 and Desk 0.38.0. Alpha, local review in progress.

An owner connects a tenant under Settings → Notifications. Agents see ordinary internal tickets labelled Falcon. Acknowledgement is explicit, queued and confirmed by reading the provider status. The Operations link requires access to the contributing Desk. Neither public screens nor employee portals receive security details. The last import time is visible. Before the first sync or after three minutes without a completed sync, current totals are unavailable.

The regression in tests/falcon.test.mjs exercises an internal ticket, denied employee access, repeated alerts, an unavailable provider, confirmation after retry and populated upgrades. PATCH simulation requires PocketIC 15 or later. Real tenant status semantics, assistive technologies and production sign-in require separate verification.

Local acceptance on 17 November: the production-matching baseline Wasms were upgraded to the stamped candidates with populated tickets. A mocked Falcon server confirmed deduplication, retry and readback, employee denial, and 1,001 additional alerts across 51 pages. The packaged Lunch regression retained the original roster and filters after Hub upgrade and six-app policy changes. The Hub owner form rendered in Chrome at 1280×800, 800×800, 320×740 and 1280×400 with no horizontal overflow. After scrolling 460 pixels on each viewport the global navigation remained at the top. The test used a synthetic backend. Touch, 200% enlargement and a screen reader were not exercised.
